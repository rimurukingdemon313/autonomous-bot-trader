**Why it failed.** The squeeze is real only in the tail: means are well above medians. But the typical coin whose
shorts were crowded kept falling against the market, and the same coins did better on days their shorts were not
crowded (every placebo draw beat the 0.03% result). Crowded shorts in crypto were mostly right, as heavily shorted
stocks underperform.

**What follows.** No later stage. The 2021-2023 spot closes are spent for rules on crowded shorts. Selling what
crowded shorts sell would be a new hypothesis formed after seeing these data, needs short selling, and carries the
squeeze tail NEWL-1 met; it is not registered.
