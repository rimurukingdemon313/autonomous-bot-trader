# Takeover audit and repair

**Final status: NO EDGE — DO NOT TRADE.**

The machine-readable record is `research_status.json`, generated by
`python scripts/research_status.py`. The README headline is derived from it,
and `--check` fails when the two disagree. If this document and that file
ever disagree, the file is right.

## 1. Executive verdict

No strategy in this repository has a defensible after-cost, out-of-sample
edge. Across every program, 38 experiments and 118 edge records have been
judged. None is VALIDATED. None is ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW. One is PROMISING,
and it is weak:

- **Turn-of-the-month on index CFDs (RCEQ-E1-TOM).** It is research only and
  not implemented in the bot.
  - After all costs it returns about 1.9–2.6% a year, with a 22–23% maximum
    drawdown at 1×.
  - Its t-statistic is 2.18–2.24, which is below the 2.5 bar.
  - At 2× costs, t falls to 0.64 and 1.36. With one session of delay it is
    1.37 and 1.51.
  - From 2021 it has t 0.46.
  - It looks decayed: t 2.75 in its development half against 0.35 in its
    validation half.
  - Even on the brief's own terms (1–3% a year with a drawdown above 20%) it
    is unattractive.

The audit also found that the repository had been reporting more than its
evidence supported:

- DIV-2's holdout was labelled VALIDATED by a "sign rule" at holdout
  t 0.26. At retail CFD costs it loses 5.7% a year (t −0.65).
- Halloween was labelled PROMISING despite failing its own preregistered
  gates.
- RV-1 was labelled PASSED with its second stage FAILED.
- Different documents gave different counts: 54, 58, 69 and 115 rejected;
  661 to 773 tests; 135 to 163 mutants.

All of these are corrected, and the counts now come from one generator.

The live path had real safety defects. All are fixed below, each with a
regression test and a mutant that the tests kill. The most serious were:

- Environment variables could loosen risk limits.
- A language model could create trades and set their stops.
- `EXPERIMENTAL_EXECUTE=true` sent unvalidated orders to a demo account.
- Exposure for yen pairs was computed in the quote currency, so about 150×
  too large.
- Neither a weekly nor an open-risk limit existed.

The system is left in a safe state. Live trading is refused. Unvalidated
trades in DEMO are shadow only. Model-proposed trades are shadow in every
mode. Per-trade risk is capped at 0.25%.

## 9. Data quality

Full report: `docs/DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.md`.

The checks found:

- 21 bar files scanned (before 2017 only).
- No duplicates, impossible OHLC bars, or ask-below-bid bars.
- **Hour 00:00–00:59 UTC is missing in 19 of 21 files.** Only EURUSD M15 and
  M5 are complete.
- Weekday gaps longer than an hour: about 2,040 per M15 file. These are that
  missing hour.

Repair was attempted:

- Dukascopy is unreachable from here (curl 000).
- The earlier runner download failed 107 of 120 requests.
- The defect therefore cannot be repaired, and the affected experiments
  cannot be rerun on corrected data.

What was done instead is to measure the bias on the one complete file:

- Removing hour 00 from EURUSD H1 changes the mean gross R of the declared
  templates by **−0.0017 to −0.0034R per trade**.
- The effect is pessimistic: a stop inside the gap fills at the 01:00 open.

**Affected experiments:**

- **M15-derived** (affected through H1/H4/D1 bars): DP-001, DP-002, SL-001,
  SL-002, CP-001, XS-001, WF-001..003, R2A–D, R3, R4, COT-1, FLOW-1 and
  PR-001.
- **M5** (affected only through ATR, Asian-range and previous-day features;
  no trade spans hour 00): ID-1 and ID-2.

None of those experiments failed by a margin anywhere near 0.0034R. The
defect cannot have turned a pass into a failure.

## 11. Deterministic (Track A) vs AI veto (Track B)

`learning/metrics.ai_veto_ab` compares the two tracks on resolved forward
EVALUATION outcomes:

- **Track A** is every deterministic decision.
- **Track B** is Track A minus the decisions the AI would have vetoed.

The rule is fixed in `AI_VETO_RULE`. The veto may enter the decision path
only if all of these hold:

- at least 100 outcomes on each side;
- the kept trades beat the vetoed ones by t ≥ 2.0;
- that also holds under 2× costs;
- it also holds in both chronological halves.

It also reports missed winners, avoided losers, and results by regime and by
symbol.

**Result: INSUFFICIENT.** The forward ledger in this repository has no
resolved EVALUATION outcomes; the deployed database is not available here.
The AI is therefore **removed from the decision path**. Its opinion is
recorded and changes nothing. A language model cannot be backtested, because
its training data contains the outcomes, so this question can only be
answered forward.

## 12. Rejected strategies and why

in `research_status.json`. They fall into five groups:

- **Negative after base costs.** SMC grades, the archive trend and

- **Positive gross, negative net.** RV-1 stage 2, DIV-2 at retail costs,
  ID-2 ridge/trees (gross +0.08 to +0.19R; net short of significance).
- **Failed preregistered gates.** Halloween.
- **Holdout not significant.** Trend edges with holdout t < 2.5, DIV-2-H.
- **Inconclusive or abandoned.** PR-001x, the archive SMC UTC rerun. Not
  counted as evidence either way.

## 13. Promising strategies and what remains unproven

**RCEQ-E1-TOM** is the only PROMISING entry. What remains unproven:

- t ≥ 2.5. It has 2.18 and 2.24.
- Survival at 2× costs, and with one session of delay, at a significant
  level.
- Persistence. The recent sub-periods are flat, and the edge decayed between
  development and validation.
- Retail index-CFD costs at the actual broker. These are unmeasured.
- Forward performance. TOM-F is registered for forward data only; no forward
  data exists yet.
- The gates nobody has measured: median trade, profit concentration,
  baseline comparison, parameter and feature stability, independent review,
  clean reproduction, demo consistency, and a human promotion record.

It is **not implemented in the bot** and must not be.

## 15. What must remain disabled

- **Live trading.** `LIVE_TRADING=true` and `MODE=LIVE` are refused;
  `REQUIRE_DEMO` has no override.
- **`EXPERIMENTAL_EXECUTE`.** Refused.
- **Execution of model-originated trades** (`llm_trader`, `trading_room`,
  `experimental_ai`). SHADOW in every mode and refused again by the
  execution engine.
- **The LLM veto.** Advisory until `AI_VETO_RULE` is met on forward data.
- **Risk above 0.25% per trade, 2% a day, 4% a week, or 0.75% open.**
  Environment variables can only tighten these.
- **`edges` mode orders.** Nothing is promoted.
- **TOM, or any PROMISING strategy, as a live strategy.**
- **Kelly, confidence-based sizing, martingale, and averaging down.** These
  do not exist, and `test_risk_only_ever_decreases_after_losses` guards it.
